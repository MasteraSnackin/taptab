## Edit rules

- Start on the working bill, not a logo animation. Prefer one continuous screen
  recording, with short punch-ins to the control being used.
- Keep the **Preview** and local-sample labels visible whenever practical.
- Burn in every subtitle cue below. Use no more than two lines, a high-contrast
  background and the lower safe area without covering the active control.
- Keep a quiet music bed under the narration. Use restrained interface sounds:
  a soft claim click, approval tick, funding pulse, settlement chime and refund
  return. Duck all music and effects beneath speech.
- At the outcome fork, both branches must visibly carry the banner **Same
  approved bill · £53.95**. Capture each branch from a reset of the same sample
  state; do not change the receipt, claims, tip or participants.
