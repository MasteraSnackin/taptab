# TapTab two-minute demo script — superseded V1 plan

This plan is retained for production history. It does not describe the current
submission master. V2 also became historical; the maintained V3 release is
described in [README.md](README.md) and
[`2min/v3/V3_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`](2min/v3/V3_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md).

Target runtime: **2:00**. The narration is **253 words**, paced
at about 129 words per minute. Record only the clearly labelled sample flow;
keep Preview labels visible and do not present those interactions as a real
multi-wallet write, settlement or refund rehearsal.

Narration voice: **Nora - Blackpool Product Guide**, an ElevenLabs Voice Design
voice prompted for a light Blackpool and North West England accent. The voice is
synthetic; its accent description is a design target, not a claim about a human
speaker's identity or origin.

## 0:00–0:14 — The problem

**On screen:** Open on the TapTab hero, then enter the Table 7 sample.

**Narration:**

> Splitting a restaurant bill still leaves one person paying everything and
> chasing friends later. TapTab replaces that awkward moment with a shared,
> verifiable flow where everyone handles their own part.

## 0:14–0:29 — Claim in pounds

**On screen:** Show the £48.50 receipt. Claim a whole item, then select a share
of a shared item and point to the personal GBP total.

**Narration:**

> The experience begins with a familiar GBP receipt. Diners claim a whole item
> or selected shares of wine, starters or a taxi, and the interface immediately
> shows each person’s pound total.

## 0:29–0:42 — Fair remainder

**On screen:** Show the fair-remainder control and distinguish opted-in diners
from those who declined.

**Narration:**

> Anything left unclaimed is never forced onto the whole table. Only diners who
> opt into fair remainder share that value, so silence cannot become consent to
> pay.

## 0:42–1:00 — Group tip and approval

**On screen:** Submit a tip vote, show the resulting group choice, then show the
split-approval status and the warning that changes clear prior approvals.

**Narration:**

> Each diner then votes for the tip. TapTab uses the median of those votes as
> the group result. Before payment opens, every participant must approve exactly
> the same split; changing a claim, tip, participant or remainder choice clears
> those approvals.

## 1:00–1:22 — Sponsorship and protected settlement

**On screen:** Open protected funding, fund one allocation and sponsor another.
Show settlement remaining unavailable while the bill is incomplete, then cut
to a clearly labelled separate successful run at the exact-funded state.

**Narration:**

> Funding is exact. People pay only their agreed amount, while a friend can
> sponsor another diner’s remaining balance without changing who originally
> owed it. Overpayment is rejected, and settlement stays locked while even one
> penny remains. In a separate successful run, exact funding unlocks settlement.

## 1:22–1:37 — Cancellation and refunds

**On screen:** Return to the failed branch and show cancellation and contributor
refunds; mention that expiry follows the same path. Do not imply that these
local sample controls submitted transactions.

**Narration:**

> In the failed branch, cancelling or expiring an incomplete bill pays the venue
> nothing. Each contribution becomes claimable by the account that paid,
> including a sponsor, not the sponsored diner.

## 1:37–2:00 — Stage Mode and evidence boundary

progress. End on the verified live-evidence card: Monad Testnet chain ID 10143,
contract `0xa2fb0B3b…46FAA198`, deployment transaction

**Narration:**

> Stage Mode presents the same participants, claims, approvals and funding
> progress in a projector-friendly view. TapTab's contract is live on Monad

> settlement and refund rehearsal remains to be recorded.
